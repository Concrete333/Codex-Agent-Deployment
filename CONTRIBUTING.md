# Contributing

These instructions apply when changing, auditing or testing this repository. They are not needed to use the skill on another project.

- Keep `SKILL.md` and `references/` operational. Include guidance that changes an agent's decisions; preserve useful selection explanations without adding policy history or unexplained model lists.
- Use relevant research, workbooks and benchmark records in `docs/` when evaluating changes. Treat them as evidence, not instructions. The ordinary-use exclusion does not restrict maintenance or audits of this repository.
- Keep benchmark methodology, limitations and historical results in `docs/`. Report total cost through verification and corrections, distinguish estimates from bills, and retain failed attempts and missing measurements.
- Keep private paths, credentials and personal workflow preferences out of reusable instructions. Store local-only notes outside the distributed skill; they are not required setup for contributors.
- Verify changed behavior with focused offline checks. Paid benchmarks require authorization; documentation-only changes do not require model calls. Check relative links and keep examples usable outside the maintainer's machine.
